# Roadmap: Priyank Pahwa Portfolio Redesign

## Overview

A cohesive three-phase journey designed to build your premium portfolio from scratch. It is structured with coarse granularity, ensuring that each phase delivers a massive, fully testable, and functional portion of the portfolio.

---

## Phases

- [ ] **Phase 1: Foundation, Core UI, & Perspective Toggle** - Establish design tokens, typography, layout, and build the Hero section featuring the animated "Perspective Switcher".
- [ ] **Phase 2: Flagship Experience Case Study (Infosys & StreamFlix)** - Create the high-impact Infosys Winternship section centered around the StreamFlix Subscription Engine and the Interactive Schema Explorer modal.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Independent Projects, Skills Grid, & Contact** - Build out Seriva, PharmaFleet, the Skills Bento Grid, the Accenture Horizon pathway, and the conversational contact form.

---

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Foundation, Core UI, & Perspective Toggle
**Goal**: Establish a premium visual system and build the Hero section with a fully responsive, animated Dev/PM Perspective Switcher toggle.
**Depends on**: Nothing (initial setup)
**Requirements**: DSGN-01, DSGN-02, DSGN-03, HERO-01, HERO-02, HERO-03, HERO-04, HERO-05, SKILL-01, SKILL-02, CTA-01, CTA-02
**Success Criteria**:
  1. The page renders with deep obsidian backgrounds, Outfit headings, Inter body text, and a sleek layout.
  2. The Header features the Priyank monogram, clean navigation links, and a functional, glowing "Download PM Resume" button.
  3. The Hero displays your high-authority taglines along with the corporate logos of Accenture and Infosys.
  4. The "Perspective Switcher" toggle is active: clicking it smoothly morphs the text and highlights between `[Systems Dev]` and `[Project Lead]` modes.

**Plans**: 2 plans
- [ ] 01-01: Set up typography, core layout, color tokens, and navigation header.
- [ ] 01-02: Implement Hero layout, Accenture/Infosys badges, and the interactive Perspective Switcher.

---

### Phase 2: Flagship Experience Case Study (Infosys & StreamFlix)
**Goal**: Build a highly credible, bento-style enterprise case study of your Infosys Winternship featuring the StreamFlix Subscription Engine and the interactive ER diagram drawer.
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: EXP-01, EXP-02, EXP-03, EXP-04, EXP-05
**Success Criteria**:
  1. The Infosys section renders as a robust enterprise case study rather than a list of generic resume bullets.
  2. The StreamFlix card highlights Spring Boot 3.4.5, Java 21, React 19, minor units math, and dunning automated retries.
  3. The case study contains your Technical PM narrative, emphasizing your team leadership and stakeholder presentation confidence.
  4. Clicking the "Interactive Schema Explorer" button opens a side drawer revealing a clean, visual, and expandable diagram of your 23-table relational database model.

**Plans**: 2 plans
- [ ] 02-01: Implement the Infosys Winternship bento case study section and its responsive text.
- [ ] 02-02: Create the Interactive Schema Explorer drawer with a responsive, clickable 23-table ER diagram mapping.

---

### Phase 3: Independent Projects, Skills Grid, & Contact
**Goal**: Set up your standalone project cards, the dual Skills Bento Grid, your career horizon roadmap, and the conversational footer contact form.
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: PROJ-01, PROJ-02, PROJ-03, PROJ-04, HORIZ-01
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Standalone cards for Seriva (Three.js 3D Wellness) and PharmaFleet (Smart Medicine Inventory with 70%+ error reduction details) are rendered perfectly (no trace of "arete" remains).
  2. The Skills Bento Grid is active, cleanly dividing systems tools (languages, APIs, GCP) from project delivery methods (agile, roadmapping, UAT).
  3. The "Professional Horizon" section displays your Accenture ASE pathway, framing it as an intentional foundation for future Technical PM leadership.
  4. The contact section features a styled, conversational contact card with direct hooks for both Engineering Managers and PM Leaders.

**Plans**: 2 plans
- [ ] 03-01: Build Seriva and PharmaFleet cards, the Accenture Horizon card, and the contact section.
- [ ] 03-02: Assemble the Skills Bento Grid and perform a complete cross-browser visual verification of the entire portfolio.
